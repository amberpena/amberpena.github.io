# Amber Peña Portfolio Design Plan

## Portfolio Goal

Create a professional website that helps Amber Peña land her first technology role by demonstrating how she uses technology to solve real business problems.

## Primary Message

Amber bridges business needs and technology through data, automation, product thinking, and user-centered design.

## Target Audience

- Technical recruiters
- Energy recruiters
- Hiring managers
- Business leaders
- AI and automation teams
- Product management teams
- UI/UX teams

## Professional Brand

Business Technology Solutions

## Homepage Headline

Building technology that solves real business problems.

## Homepage Introduction

I combine business analysis, data, automation, product strategy, and thoughtful design to create practical technology solutions. With experience in the energy industry and a background in Computer Science and Management Information Systems, I enjoy turning complex problems into useful, user-centered experiences.

2. About
3. Featured Work
4. Case Studies

7. Resume
8. Contact
