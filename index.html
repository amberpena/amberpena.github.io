Featured analytics project

Natural Gas Market Intelligence.

A business-intelligence project that transforms nearly three decades of Henry Hub natural gas spot price data into a clear, readable market overview.

Turning historical energy data into accessible market insight.

I designed this Power BI dashboard using monthly Henry Hub Natural Gas Spot Price data from the U.S. Energy Information Administration. The goal was to create a focused executive-style report that makes long-term pricing behavior, volatility, and major historical movements easier to understand.

Download Power BI file ↓ View dashboard image ↗
My role Data Analyst and Dashboard Designer
Tools Power BI, Power Query and Excel
Data source U.S. Energy Information Administration
Business focus Pricing Trends and Market Intelligence
01 · Business question

How has the Henry Hub spot price changed over time?

Historical pricing files contain valuable market information, but raw tables can make it difficult to quickly identify long-term patterns. This dashboard was created to provide an immediate view of price movement, historical highs and lows, and overall average market behavior.

02 · Analytics process

From public data to business insight.

I followed a practical analytics workflow to collect, clean, organize, and visualize the monthly pricing information.

01

Collect

Downloaded monthly Henry Hub natural gas spot price data published by the EIA.

02

Transform

Used Power Query to remove unnecessary rows, rename fields, and organize the source data.

03

Validate

Converted the date and pricing columns into usable data types and reviewed the final dataset for consistency.

04

Visualize

Built KPI cards and a time-series chart to communicate the most important market information.

03 · Dashboard metrics

Key figures presented at a glance.

The dashboard uses summary cards to make the highest, lowest, and average monthly prices immediately visible before the user explores the full historical trend.

What the trend makes visible.

Periods of major volatility

The visual makes the sharp price movements during the 2000s and early 2020s immediately recognizable.

Long periods of lower pricing

The chart also reveals extended periods when monthly prices remained closer to the lower end of the historical range.

Clearer executive communication

KPI cards provide immediate context while the line chart preserves the full historical story.

Featured Python project

Clean Slate Juice Ordering System.

Turning a real business idea into working Python logic.

I created a console-based ordering system for Clean Slate Juice to explore how programming can improve a real customer-ordering process. The application guides customers through product selection, quantities, discounts, bottle returns, and final checkout calculations.

My role Designer, Developer and Product Owner
Technologies Python, VS Code and GitHub
Project type Console Application
Business focus Ordering, Pricing and Customer Experience
01 · Challenge

Create one clear ordering flow.

A juice-ordering experience needs to do more than calculate a price. It must guide customers through the menu, prevent invalid entries, apply promotions correctly, and provide an easy-to-read summary before the transaction is completed.

02 · Solution

Business rules transformed into application features.

I broke the ordering process into reusable functions and decision points so the application could handle customer choices, pricing rules, rewards, and validation consistently.

01

Interactive menu

Displays juice names, ingredients, prices, and numbered product selections.

02

Input validation

Prevents invalid product numbers, nonnumeric inputs, and quantities below one.

03

Multiple products

Allows customers to continue adding different juices before completing the order.

04

Wellness messages

Provides a product-specific wellness message after each selected juice.

05

Bundle pricing

Automatically applies seven-bottle pricing when a customer reaches the bundle quantity.

06

First-order reward

Applies a first-time buyer discount when the customer confirms it is their first order.

07

Bottle returns

Calculates a reusable-bottle return credit and subtracts it from the final amount.

08

Order summary

Presents quantities, item totals, discounts, credits, subtotals, and the final total.

03 · Program output

A completed customer order.

This demonstration shows a customer selecting a product, entering a quantity, receiving a wellness message, applying a first-order discount, returning a bottle, and receiving a final itemized total.
